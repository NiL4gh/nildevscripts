2.2 Critical Pre-Flight Checklist

  • Text Expansion: Verify all typography is converted to outlines (Ctrl+Shift+O / Cmd+Shift+O) to prevent font missing rejections.
  • Path Hygiene: Eliminate zero-length paths, open outlines, and unpainted stray points.
  • Color Space Integrity: Ensure swatches are strictly in RGB or CMYK per target marketplace specifications.
  • Artboard Padding: Auto-fit canvas bounding box to prevent clipping delicate drop shadows or outer glow blurs.

Installation & Setup

Copy the script file (e.g. StockVector Exporter Pro (Exchange).jsx) into your Adobe Illustrator Scripts directory, then restart Illustrator. The script appears under File > Scripts.

WINDOWS SCRIPTS DIRECTORY:

C:\Program Files\Adobe\Adobe Illustrator [Version]\Presets\[Language]\Scripts\

macOS SCRIPTS DIRECTORY:

/Applications/Adobe Illustrator [Version]/Presets/[Language]/Scripts/

License & Activation

Your license key is delivered to the email address used at checkout (it is also shown in your purchase library). Activation takes seconds and is verified against a secure licensing server.

  1. Install the script into your Illustrator Scripts directory and restart Illustrator.
  2. Run StockVector Exporter Pro from File > Scripts.
  3. Open the activation dialog inside the panel and paste your license key.
  4. The key is verified instantly; your seats are cached locally for offline use.
  5. To move machines, deactivate a seat with 1 click in the script UI and activate on the new computer.

Offline use: you only need an internet connection once when activating your license key. After activation, the seat is cryptographically cached on your machine, allowing full offline batch processing without ongoing internet access.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do seat activations work? +
An Individual license includes 2 simultaneous seat activations (e.g. your desktop workstation and laptop). A Team license includes 20 simultaneous activations. You can easily deactivate and transfer your seats to a new computer with 1 click directly inside the script UI.
Does the script work offline? +
Yes! You only need an internet connection once when activating your license key. After activation, the seat is cryptographically cached on your machine, allowing full offline batch processing without ongoing internet access.

StockVector Exporter Pro is built on pure ECMAScript 3 (ES3) ExtendScript and is compatible with Adobe Illustrator CC 2019 through Illustrator 2026+ on both Windows 10/11 and macOS (Intel & Apple Silicon M1/M2/M3/M4).

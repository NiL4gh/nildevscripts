Installation & Setup

Copy the script file (StockVector Exporter Pro.jsxbin) into your Adobe Illustrator Scripts directory:

WINDOWS SCRIPTS DIRECTORY:

C:\Program Files\Adobe\Adobe Illustrator [Version]\Presets\[Language]\Scripts\

macOS SCRIPTS DIRECTORY:

/Applications/Adobe Illustrator [Version]/Presets/[Language]/Scripts/

Frequently Asked Questions

How do seat activations work? +
An Individual license includes 2 simultaneous seat activations (e.g. your desktop workstation and laptop). A Team license includes 10 simultaneous activations. You can easily deactivate and transfer your seats to a new computer with 1 click directly inside the script UI.
Does the script work offline? +
Yes! You only need an internet connection once when activating your license key. After activation, the seat is cryptographically cached on your machine, allowing full offline batch processing without ongoing internet access.
Which Adobe Illustrator versions are supported? +
StockVector Exporter Pro is built on pure ECMAScript 3 (ES3) ExtendScript and is compatible with Adobe Illustrator CC 2019 through Illustrator 2026+ on both Windows 10/11 and macOS (Intel & Apple Silicon M1/M2/M3/M4).
